## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: transparent raster illustration for an academic personal website's small clickable "gold nuggets" feature; intended display width 170px, preferably deliver a compact 512px UI asset.
Primary request: A beautifully tactile cluster of exactly three small natural rough gold nuggets, one main nugget and two smaller companion chunks. An editorial mineral-study art object with subtly painterly photographic realism, understated and distinctive, like an intriguing curiosity on an academic's desk.
Scene/backdrop: Genuine fully transparent background with actual alpha, including clear transparency around and between the nuggets. The object should sit naturally with only a very subtle small semitransparent contact shadow. No ground plane, no backdrop color.
Composition/framing: Low wide cluster, landscape 3:2 canvas. Entire cluster centered and fully visible, occupying about 80–85% of canvas width with minimal empty top and bottom padding. Main nugget is irregular and craggy, with two smaller chunks nestled low on either side; natural varied silhouettes, not geometric gemstones. Clear readable silhouette at 170px.
Lighting/mood: Soft directional studio highlights from upper left; warm, precious, quiet and scholarly rather than flashy.
Color palette: Muted Purdue gold #CFB991 highlights, warm brass and ochre midtones, deep brown shading; restrained luminous metallic glints, never neon yellow.
Materials/textures: Rough native gold with craggy crystalline facets, fine pitted surface, small crevices, believable metallic density and rich tactile detail.
Constraints: Produce a standalone PNG with a real alpha channel; all blank canvas must have alpha zero. No checkerboard pattern baked into pixels. No text, lettering, logo, watermark, frame, coins, bars, cut gemstones, treasure chest, extra objects, UI elements, background scene or solid color background.
